Keyboard shortcuts

    
Click a shortcut, then press the new keys. Esc cancels. Shortcuts need Ctrl/Cmd or Alt, except function keys.

    
    

    

      
      On macOS, menu shortcuts update after Island restarts.
    

  


  

    
Import from another browser

    
Bring your bookmarks over, or recreate your Arc spaces and pinned tabs. Nothing leaves this computer.

    

  


  

    
Updates

    
Island checks its GitHub releases for new versions. Every download is checked against its published SHA-256 checksum before it is installed.
