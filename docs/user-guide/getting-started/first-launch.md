# First launch

The first time you open Island, a **Welcome to Island** card asks two things: how Island should look, and whether to bring your bookmarks along.

## Choose a theme

Under **Appearance**, pick **System**, **Light** or **Dark**. System follows your operating system.

The default look is **Native**: a macOS-style source-list sidebar with the system font and one blue accent. You can switch to the Arc or Graphite look later in [Appearance](../settings/appearance.md).

Native shows the welcome as a rounded card over a dimmed page. Its theme choices use the same
neutral segmented control as the sidebar.

## Import bookmarks

Under **Import**, Island lists the browsers it found on this computer. It can read bookmarks from Chrome-family browsers, Safari and Firefox, and the spaces and pinned tabs of Arc. One Chrome-family row imports the combined bookmarks from its readable profiles.

1. Tick the browsers you want to import.
2. Click **Import selected**. To skip importing, click **Start browsing**.

Nothing leaves your computer. Browsers without a local profile are not listed; a detected profile
that Island cannot open is shown with the specific reason. A profile with no bookmarks is not
listed. If Island finds no readable bookmarks, the card says so. Firefox can be open while
importing: Island reads a temporary copy of its profile database and its current changes.

Imported bookmarks show up in the [command palette](../search/command-palette.md). Arc spaces become Island spaces with their colors and pinned tabs.

## Open the card again

On macOS, choose **Browser > Show Welcome…**. You can also import later in **Settings > General > Import from another browser**.

## What you see next

A new tab opens on the [start page](../search/start-page.md). The sidebar lists your tabs and spaces.

To learn the keys, press ⌘/ (Ctrl+/ on Windows and Linux). A card in the bottom-right corner lists the ten most-used shortcuts. Press ⌘/ or Esc again to close it. The full list is in [Keyboard shortcuts](../reference/keyboard-shortcuts.md).
