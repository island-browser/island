# Appearance

Open **Settings** with ⌘, (Ctrl+,), then choose **Appearance**. Every change applies right away to every window. **Reset to defaults** undoes them.

## Look

**Look** is a preset: **Native**, **Arc** or **Graphite**. The default is Native. If you change any other control below, the preset reads **Custom**. See [The sidebar](../basics/sidebar.md) for what each look is.

## Theme

**Theme** follows the system, or is always **Light** or **Dark**. The options are **Match system**, **Light** and **Dark**.

## Color

These controls apply to the Arc and Graphite looks. **Native has no space color**, so Island hides them there.

* **Space colors**: each space tints the sidebar with its own color. Pick one of ten presets or type a hex color such as `#7C5CFF`.
* **Sidebar tint**: how strongly the space color washes the sidebar and the window margin.

## Layout

* **Sidebar density**: **Comfortable** or **Compact**.
* **Sidebar position**: **Left** or **Right**.
* **Page corner radius**: 0, 6, 10 or 14 (macOS).
* **Full bleed**: the page fills its side edge to edge, with no inset, rounding or shadow.
* **Page card shadow**: one soft shadow under the page card.

## Text

**Interface font**: **Geist** (bundled), **System** (the platform font), or **Custom family**. For a custom family, type the name of any installed font family.

## Good to know

* The rounded, shadowed page card is **macOS only**. Other platforms keep square corners and the platform window frame.
* The frameless Arc window, with the traffic lights over the sidebar, is also macOS only.
