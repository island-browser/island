WORKSPACE NOTE

Keep the thread.

One browser surface, arranged around the work you mean to return to.

Today’s focus

Future product concept. The tabs, spaces, split view, and command surface shown here are not shipping. Today’s macOS build is an empty native-window foundation.

Built with intention

The web has enough noise. A browser can make room for thought.

Island explores a quieter model: familiar browsing, organized by the work in front of you rather than by a growing pile of tabs.

A direction, not a release note

Designed around context.

The following are product concepts under active development. They are not promises of currently available functionality.

Spaces

Separate the work, not just the windows.

Spaces are a proposed way to keep project, research, and personal browsing distinct—without turning each into another inbox.
